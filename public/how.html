How does this work?

  1. Copy the prompt. Click Copy prompt in the app (or open PROMPT.md and copy it) and paste it into any AI chat.
  2. Give it your CV. Paste your existing CV text, a LinkedIn dump, or just notes — plus your preferences (colors, photo side, font, paper size).
  3. Get JSON back. The AI replies with a single JSON object matching cv.schema.json.
  4. Paste the JSON into step 2 of the app. Add your photo in step 3 if the JSON references one.
  5. Preview. The CV renders live in the panel on the right — real A4/Letter pages with real page breaks.
  6. Export. Click Export → PDF. Your browser's print dialog opens. Choose “Save as PDF”, margins None, background graphics on. Download your vector PDF.

What the JSON controls

Everything visual comes from the JSON the AI writes — the app has no style settings of its own.

  • Theme: accent colors, heading and body fonts, font size, density (compact/normal/airy)
  • Layout: photo left/right/top/none, side-column width, single or two-column
  • Sections: experience, education, skills, projects, certifications, languages, interests — plus free-form text blocks
  • Labels: translate section titles and “Present” into any language (Czech, German, Spanish…)

Want a different look? Ask the AI to change the JSON, paste it again, and the preview updates.

Privacy

Everything happens in your browser. The JSON, your photo, and the rendered CV never leave your device. No account, no server storage, no tracking. When you close the tab it's gone (except the draft autosave in your browser's local storage).
