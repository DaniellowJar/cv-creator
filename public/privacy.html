Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

We don't collect anything. CV Creator runs entirely in your browser.

What we collect

Nothing. No CV text, no photos, no personal data, no IP logs tied to you, no cookies, no analytics, no tracking pixels.

Where your data lives

Your draft CV is stored only in your browser's local storage (so it survives a refresh). It never leaves your device. Clearing your browser data deletes it.

Third parties

None. No ads, no trackers, no external APIs except optional Google Fonts (used for typefaces; you can swap in local font files instead).
